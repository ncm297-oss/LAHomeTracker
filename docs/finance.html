How financing changes the math

  1. With a loan you put only the down payment plus loan costs into the house. The rest of the cash stays invested at the retained-cash return (R).
  2. Owner outflow each year = property tax + insurance + maintenance + HOA + P&I − tax benefit. The tax benefit is what itemizing (capped SALT + deductible interest) saves versus the standard deduction, federal and California separately. Even a cash buyer gets the property-tax part.
  3. As in the cash calculator, whoever pays less each year invests the difference in the renter's portfolio.
  4. At sale the owner nets home proceeds − remaining loan balance + R after tax. Wealth difference = owner net − renter net.
  5. "Cash, refi" buys with cash and borrows at the end of year N; the proceeds (less refi cost) go into R and payments start the next year.
  6. Leverage only helps if the after-tax loan rate is below the after-tax return on R. The crossover panel shows both and the mortgage rate at which they tie.
